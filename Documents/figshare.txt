demoVFT columns:

site: one of 6 sites; consistent with other datasets

ID: unique ID number of the plant, which stays consistent across years. Note that ID’s can be repeated across sites.

Libsur_prevTS: survival since the previous year (1= survived, 0= died)

Size: ln of size in the current year (where size is the product of leaf number and longest leaf length, in mm)

Fr: fruit number in the current year

Year: current year

Size_prevTS: for ease of reference, ln of size in the previous year (where size is the product of leaf number and longest leaf length, in mm)

Burn: years since burn (where 1 indicates a site burned sometime over the past year)

Binrep: binary fruiting response in the current year; 0 if a plant did not fruit; 1 if it did

Region: one of three regions; each region houses two sites; consistent with other datasets

annual.precip.metdata: cumulative annual precipitation over the past year, in mm.

precip.wettest.month.metdata: cumulative precipitation in the wettest month in the past year, in mm.

precip.driest.month.metdata: cumulative precipitation in the driest month in the past year, in mm.

precip.coldest.month.metdata: cumulative precipitation in the coldest month in the past year, in mm.

precip.warmest.month.metdata: cumulative precipitation in the warmest month in the past year, in mm.

Each of the following climate variables either has an “ibutton” or “metdata” suffix. The “ibutton” suffix indicates that temperature data were drawn from ibutton temperatures (soil temperature; see main text). The “metdata” suffix indicates that temperature data were drawn from air temperatures (from METDATA; see main text).

annual.mean.temp: annual mean temperature over the past year, in degrees C

max.dmean.temp.warmest.month: maximum of daily mean temperatures during the warmest month over the past year, in degrees C

min.dmean.temp.coldest.month: minimum of daily mean temperatures in the coldest month over the past year, in degrees C.

cwd.warmest.month: climatic water deficit in the warmest month over the past year, in mm.

cwd.wettest.month: climatic water deficit in the wettest month over the past year, in mm.

cwd.driest.month: climatic water deficit in the driest month over the past year, in mm.

cwd.annual: climatic water deficit over the past year, in mm.

waterbalance.warmest.month: water balance in the warmest month over the past year, in mm/ month.

waterbalance.driest.month: water balance in the driest month over the past year, in mm/ month.

waterbalance.wettest.month: water balance in the wettest month over the past year, in mm/ month.

waterbalance.annual.ibutton: water balance over the past year, in mm/ month.

cwd.coldest.month: climatic water deficit in the coldest month over the past year, in mm.

waterbalance.coldest.month: water balance in the coldest month over the past year, in mm/ month.

mn.dmean.temp.driest.month: mean of the daily mean temperatures in the driest month over the past year, in degrees C.

mn.dmean.temp.wettest.month: mean of the daily mean temperatures in the wettest month over the past year, in degrees C.

demoVFT.rec columns:

site: one of six sites, consistent across datasets

seedlings.per.m.2: seedlings per m^2 found in the indicated year

year: year of survey

fruitperm2_lastyear : total fruits per m^2 found in the year prior

burn: years since burn (where 1 indicates a site burned sometime over the past year)

region: one of three regions; each region houses two sites; consistent with other datasets

NRVFT columns:

site: one of six sites, consistent across datasets.

binrep: binary fruiting response in the current year; 0 if a plant did not fruit; 1 if it did.

growth: log of size in the current year.

libsur_prevTS: survival since the previous year (1= survived, 0= died).

logfr_conditionalpersize: log (fruit number / size), for all plants that fruited in that year, where size is the product of leaf number and longest leaf length, in mm.

size_prevTS: log of size in the year prior (where size is the product of leaf number and longest leaf length, in mm).

trt: one of “both”,”ctrl”, “fire”, or ”rem.” “Both” treatment is neighbor removal + ash addition, “ctrl” is unmanipulated control treatment, “fire” is the accidental fire condition, and “rem” is neighbor removal.

Year: year that measurements were taken.

trt: one of “both”,”ctrl”, “fire”, or ”rem.” “Both” treatment is neighbor removal + ash addition, “ctrl” is unmanipulated control treatment, “fire” is the accidental fire condition, and “rem” is neighbor removal.
